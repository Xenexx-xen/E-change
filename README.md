# E-change
A marketplace for engineering students to buy and sell used project components.

## The Problem
Engineering students waste a lot of components on mini-projects once the semester ends. The expensive things collect dust. On the other hand, new students and juniors have problems acquiring project parts for a cost-effective price.

## The Solution
A platform where seniors can list out anything they don't want for a cheap price. 
* Seniors earn money.
* Juniors get cheap, usable items.
* No parts get wasted.

### Key Features
* **Market place:** Sellers(seniors in this case) can list out any item they want to sell. Decide their own prices. Buyers(juniors) can click on anything to buy and add to their cart.
* **Verification Seal:** Products that are tested and approved get a seal to ensure faulty products aren't passed around.
* **Customer Feedback:** An optional review section for buyers.
